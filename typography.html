04 · Governance

Non-negotiable rules

Component styling may consume these tokens. It may not silently redefine them.

check_circle

Use semantic roles

Choose a role from meaning and component context, then use its documented token.

check_circle

Keep reading text generous

Educational body text remains at least 16 px with sufficient line height and a controlled measure.

cancel

No local font inventions

A component cannot introduce an undocumented family, size, weight, tracking value or text colour.

cancel

No tiny functional text

Label small remains 14 px here. Instructions, warnings and interactive labels never use 11 or 12 px.
